Our Culinary Heritage

Crafted by Fire, Guided by Memory.

NEW PEARL was conceived as an architectural tribute to the forgotten hearths of the Indian subcontinent. We do not reinterpret Indian food to dilute it; we intensify its ancestral soul through the lens of modern gastronomic precision.

Every dish is an exploration of micro-terroirs: black cardamom cured in the misty foothills of Sikkim, cold-pressed mustard oil from Morena, and slow wood-fire embers kept alive for thirty-six continuous hours. Our culinary team blends centuries of royal tandoor technique with clean, unhurried contemporary aesthetics.

“

Indian cuisine is not a static monolith—it is a living, breathing symphony of spice architecture, smoke, and patience.

Chef Anirudh Sen Culinary Director & Founder

Masterpiece Feature
Vegetarian Heirloom Heritage Awadh & Moradabad

24-Hour Smoked Dal Moradabadi & Truffle Warqi Kulcha

₹ 950 Exclusive Tasting Presentation

Heirloom yellow moong lentils simmered over dying teak embers for twenty-four continuous hours. Finished with clarified churned butter, roasted cumin crisps, raw mango reduction, and accompanied by flaky multi-layered warqi kulcha infused with black winter truffle.

  • Smoke Intensity: Subtle Babool charcoal infusion
  • Texture: Velvety slow-melt consistency with crisp jaggery-cumin pearls
  • Finish: Earthy truffle warmth with a bright citrus linger

Our moong lentils are harvested by a regenerative farming collective in Vidarbha, roasted lightly in stone pans before their slow immersion in copper deghs. Our truffles are sustainably foraged and steeped into cultured A2 cow butter.

Sommelier Pick: 2021 Sula Dindori Reserve Viognier or our signature Smoked Vetiver Old Fashioned, accented with Himalayan wild honey and oak bitters.
